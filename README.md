### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a UAE-based software engineer with **4+ years of experience**, mainly focused on frontend and full-stack development. I've worked on production websites, web applications, eCommerce systems, marketing platforms, dashboards, and browser-based games.

I like building things end to end — from the interface and APIs to databases, integrations, deployment, performance, and maintenance.

### 🚀 Projects I Built

#### 🎮 [LevelPort](https://levelport.dev) — A space for indie game creators

A platform where indie game developers, designers, and artists can **showcase games, share updates, discover projects, and build their portfolios**.

- Designed and developed the platform from scratch
- Built game publishing, discovery, social features, and creator profiles
- Worked on authentication, database architecture, dashboards, and the UI/UX
- Bringing game discovery and a developer community into one product

**Built with:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `PostgreSQL`

[![Explore LevelPort](https://img.shields.io/badge/Explore-LevelPort.dev-A98263?style=for-the-badge&logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white)](https://levelport.dev)

#### 📣 [OttaBlast](https://ottablast.com) — Multi-channel marketing SaaS

A marketing platform that helps businesses manage **WhatsApp campaigns, email blasts, leads, and scheduled outreach** from one place.

- Developed the frontend, backend, and dashboard workflows
- Built lead management, campaign scheduling, and reusable templates
- Integrated email delivery, WhatsApp messaging, payments, and AI services
- Worked on APIs, automation, infrastructure, and deployment

**Built with:** `React` `Laravel` `MySQL` `Amazon SES` `WhatsApp API` `Stripe` `Gemini API`

[![Explore OttaBlast](https://img.shields.io/badge/Explore-OttaBlast.com-607D8B?style=for-the-badge&logo=googlechrome&logoColor=white)](https://ottablast.com)
